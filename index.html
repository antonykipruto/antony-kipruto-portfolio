How I work

Travel planning is more than making a reservation. It involves understanding the traveller, checking the details and coordinating the parts of the journey.

I begin by understanding the purpose of travel, destination preferences, dates, budget and any specific requirements. I then help identify suitable options and explain the relevant details so the client can decide how to proceed. For more complex trips, this can mean coordinating several flights, hotels, transfers and activities into one coherent itinerary.

Accuracy is especially important when handling passenger details, fare rules, booking conditions and visa-documentation requirements. I aim to keep records organized, communicate changes promptly and follow through on outstanding arrangements. When plans change, I focus on identifying practical alternatives and keeping the client informed.

I also value professional relationships with suppliers, accommodation providers and travel partners. Clear communication with these partners can help secure appropriate options, resolve booking questions and maintain a smoother experience for the traveller.
